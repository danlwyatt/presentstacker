Present Stacker – Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Present Stacker (“the game”) is a mobile stacking game made by Daniel Wyatt (“I” or “me”). This policy explains what information the game uses, who else handles it, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts. I don’t ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.
  • Your high score, collection, and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information to show and measure ads. You can decline tracking, and you can buy Remove Ads.
  • Apple handles all purchases. I never see your payment details.

Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following on your device so it can work between sessions:

  • your best score
  • the holiday treasures you have collected
  • how many Safety Nets and Slow-Mo Boosts you own
  • whether you have bought Remove Ads
  • whether you have already seen the instructions

This information is not sent to me or to any server I run. Deleting the game removes it. If you delete and reinstall the game, you can use Restore Purchases to get Remove Ads back. Safety Nets and Slow-Mo Boosts you have not used are stored only on your device and can’t be restored.

Ads and Google AdMob

The game shows ads between levels using Google AdMob. To show and measure those ads, Google’s software inside the game may collect or use:

  • device identifiers, including your device’s advertising identifier if you allow tracking
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • device and app details, such as the device model, operating system version, and language
  • information about which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, personalize the ads you see. How Google handles this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and on the page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

I do not receive your personal information from Google. I only see summary reports, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. You can say no and still play. To change your answer later, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Consent in Europe and the UK. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before ads are loaded. Where it is offered, you can change your choices at any time with the Privacy Settings button on the Shop screen.
  • Remove Ads. If you buy Remove Ads, the game stops showing ads, and on later launches it does not start Google’s ad software.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads, a pack of Safety Nets, and a pack of Slow-Mo Boosts. Apple processes these purchases. I never see your card details or your Apple Account password. Apple tells the game whether a purchase was completed so the game can give you what you bought. Apple’s handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Present Stacker is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given personal information through the game, please contact me and I will help.

Sharing and selling

I do not collect personal information myself, so I have none to sell or share. The only other companies involved are Google, which provides the ads, and Apple, which provides the App Store and purchases. Each handles information as described in its own policy.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stored by the game stays on your device until you delete the game. Information collected by Google is kept and can be managed under Google’s own policies, linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of personal information, or to object to how it is used. Because I do not keep personal information about you, there is usually nothing for me to look up or delete. For information collected by Google, you can use the privacy controls described in Google’s policy. You are always welcome to contact me with questions.

Security

Data saved by the game lives inside the game’s protected storage on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but the game does not send your saved data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I will change the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, I will mention them in the game’s App Store release notes.
